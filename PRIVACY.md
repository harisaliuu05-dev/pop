# Lantern Lane privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Lantern Lane is a single-player idle game.

**Your game save.** Your progress is stored on your device. On Android it is also included in Android Backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. We do not run any servers and we do not receive your save.

**Purchases.** In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

**Ads.** The free version shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Buying "No Ads" removes them.

**Notifications.** The app can send reminders that are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13.

**Contact.** For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/harisaliuu05-dev/pop/issues.
